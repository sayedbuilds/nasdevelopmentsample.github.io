Founded 2007, Muscat

We buy the tree, not the bottle.

Most attar sold today is bought finished, in drums, from a broker who will not name the forest. Qafila started because our founder could not get a straight answer about a single kilo of agarwood.

So we went to the source instead. Today we hold standing agreements with four growing regions, run our own copper stills in Salalah and Jorhat, and keep every distillation under a batch number you can look up on the vial.

Nothing leaves before it has rested a year. Fresh oil is loud; a rested oil settles into something you can wear all day.

Ahmed Al-BalushiFounder & head distiller
